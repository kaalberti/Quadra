# Operating mass follows demonstrated servo capability

Decision date:2026-10-09. User instruction:weight may be heavier as long as the
servos can handle it. The complete2kg operating mass is now a preferred target,
not a hard ceiling. Cost/simplicity remain priorities; no actuator upgrade or
new numerical maximum is approved by this change.

Maximum acceptable operating mass stays TBD until supported leg tests and
whole-robot standing/slow-motion tests demonstrate useful torque,current,
heating and duty behavior at actual voltage,geometry and usable ranges.
Weighing a robot or comparing against stall torque does not qualify that mass.
Battery/electronics/wiring still count in complete operating mass.

The active integrated estimate remains2.178kg with provisional print/hardware
and power allowances. It is above the preferred target,not automatically
rejected. No measured masses,servo limits or physical load results are added.
Existing2kg torque cases remain reference calculations. Do not retrofit old
archived reports as qualifications for a heavier robot.

Consequences:mass reports separate target margins from a qualified limit,
which is currently null. Printed mounts may favor simpler conservative parts;
do not force weight reduction solely to meet2kg. Retain MG996R provisionally,
5.2V bench rail,70/85mm links,narrow bench commands and physical progression.
