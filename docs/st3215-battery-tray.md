# ST3215 battery tray

User-provided provisional pack envelope:120 x50 x20 mm,60 Wh conventional3S LiPo.
Source:mechanical/st3215-battery-tray.scad; printable STL alongside it.
Pocket122 x52 mm allows1 mm per side;134 x66 mm overall,3 mm floor,8 mm side
retention above floor. Add1 mm soft padding beneath pack. No bolt head should
contact the pouch; four mounting holes are outside its nominal footprint.
Use two15 mm-wide reusable straps,about250 mm or longer with adequate overlap,
through the four16 x3.5 mm slots. Secure snugly without compressing cells.
Smooth slot edges,protect pouch from rough prints and leave cable/balance leads
accessible. Tray is open/removable; do not charge the pack installed in robot.

Mount low and centrally under/within the eventual chassis. The full robot and
new deck have not been revised,so tray mounting coordinates are not a released
chassis interface. Check leg sweep and ground clearance once chassis is laid out.
Pack mass,connector,C rating,condition and exact shape remain TBD. Confirm the
actual pack including lead exit/strain relief before final print/mounting.
