# MEC-162 — physical integration of experimental pitch forks

Completed2026-10-09. Both source/placed checks pass (maximum vertex difference
0.001005mm, within ASCII STL rounding allowance); all five sampled hip/carrier
intersections are empty. Rendered full assembly visually inspected. No geometry
correction or conditional BOM/mass change was needed.

The experimental three-DOF viewer is
`mechanical/prototype-fork-leg-assembly.scad`, with a rendered PNG alongside it.
Its generated `fork-leg-parts.scad` retains the supported bench's physical print
placements, replacing six pitch-output pieces with two integrated forks.
There are25 printed pieces including the bench adapter, down from29.

Both forks use the mirrored physical STL for this bench hand. The placement
matrix composes the intended pitch frame, the print's own mirror and the inverse
side-on print rotation. The resulting matrix has determinant+1. Reflection is
used to manufacture the opposite hand, never to pretend a part can be reflected
in assembly. Other pieces retain their already checked rigid bench poses.

Regenerate with `node mechanical/build-fork-leg.mjs`. This verifies baseline
and fork STL hashes before generating the viewer and placement record. Joint
parameters in `fork_leg(q1,q2,q3)` are geometric trial angles only; there is no
firmware linkage or approval for powered motion.

`node mechanical/check-fork-leg.mjs` independently exports each placed STL and
its intended source-CAD shape, comparing bidirectional vertices within0.002mm
and relative volumes within0.0001. It then checks new fork intersections with
J1 fixed prints/case/bench adapter and moving arms/retainer/carrier at nominal
and four combinations of the trial endpoints. Results and input hashes are in
`mechanical/fork-leg-check.json`. These are sampled checks, not a continuous
sweep, actual servo ear/horn/lead fit, or strength validation.

The conditional MEC-161 BOM/mass estimate remains unchanged unless a clearance
correction is needed. The supported bench-3 manufacturing kit remains intact;
the experimental viewer does not create a new released parts kit.

Physical print/support removal, bearing-seat/horn/retainer assembly and gradually
loaded leg tests remain unperformed. Do not infer these results from CAD.
