# MEC-160 — working robot mass screen

Added mechanical/robot-mass-inputs.json with null measured/sliced records for
all22 working STLs, actual component measurements and complete robot weight.
check-robot-working-mass.mjs verifies hashes/117-piece quantities and separates
solid CAD volume, assumed print mass, estimated hardware and measured values.
Real status remains NOT_MEASURED.

At1.27g/cm3 PETG density, solid prints total1301.97g; the65% material-use
example gives846.28g. Hardware estimate483.91g,12x55g servos660g,12 reference
bearings39.60g and330g provisional battery/electronics/wiring/misc allowances
give2359.79g,359.79g above the2kg limit. All prints have486.49g available under
those other assumptions,37.37% of solid CAD mass.65% is not infill percentage.

Published references checked publicly: TowerPro55g servo, SKF3.3g624-2RS1
reference, Accu M3x40 dimensions/2.36g weight. The envelope/thread estimate
gives2.369g for that screw. Other fastener geometry remains explicitly rough;
three existing CAD-volume reports independently agree within0.01mm3.

Decision: retain the supported bench kit for proving one leg, but do not treat
its complete replication as a demonstrated2kg robot. Prototype a simpler pitch
output fork to remove long bolts and separate pieces together; this alone is
not claimed to recover the entire excess. No detailed stress/optimization work,
premium component, purchase, supplier contact or measured fit claim is added.
The manufacturing checkpoint remains unchanged and passes its53-file check.
