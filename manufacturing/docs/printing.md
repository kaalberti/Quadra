# Printing — integrated-bench-1

Use [the print list](print-list.md) and [the side-on coupon guide](fork-fit-coupon.md).
The23-piece assembly has15 unique STLs; five coupons are separate optional
fit checks. Start with the small side-on fork coupon before either full fork.
Use access to the K2 Pro; all supplied meshes fit within a300mm bed.

Keep each STL in its exported orientation at z=0. The integrated hip's carrier
face rests on the bed, with its output rings upright; the pitch forks stand
side-on with their web lands on the bed. Do not rotate the rings flat simply
to avoid supports: it changes the layer orientation being tested. Use the
original hip and mirrored upper/lower forks and saddle listed in this pack.

Use PLA+/PETG with your established material preset. Provisional starting
settings are0.4mm nozzle,0.2mm layers, four walls and30% infill. These are
starting process assumptions, not verified strength settings. Inspect every
slice before printing. Add a brim if needed and provide accessible supports
under the hip's raised rings, fork curved plates, horizontal bores and any
unsupported overhangs. Keep supports removable from bearing seats, horn slots
and fixing holes; inspect for sagging and trapped support after removal.
There are no claimed support-free or slicer-verified results.

Check the cradle coupon against the actual servo, bearing coupon seats
(1/2/3 dots indicate13.0/13.2/13.4mm), horn coupon and knee-ear coupon. The
new side-on coupon tests the actual fork bearing/horn geometry in its print
orientation; its connector is not a full-fork strength test. Use the same
material/layer/wall/support approach for the subsequent complete fork.

Record material, actual slicer settings, support removal, mass, horn seating,
bearing/retainer fit and bolt access. Do not glue a bearing or force a tight
servo into place before assessing fit. Reject cracks, damaged seat lips and
unremovable supports. Prototype testing, rather than further detailed stress
analysis, determines whether revisions are needed.

All prints and physical tests remain NOT_PERFORMED. The previous bench-3 pack
is archived intact as a fallback. Print and assemble one leg before considering
bulk robot purchases; complete robot2kg compliance is not established.
